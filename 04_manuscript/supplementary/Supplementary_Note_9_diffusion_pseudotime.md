# Supplementary Note 9 — Expression-derived diffusion pseudotime rooted at nerve spots

**Rationale.** If the perineural niche were merely a smooth mixture of cell types, spots would have no preferred ordering. A pseudotime built from expression alone — with no spatial input — that nevertheless orders spots along physical distance is a non-trivial consistency check.

**Construction (per sample).** Spots with a finite nerve distance retained; counts library-normalised to 10,000 and log1p-transformed; 2,000 highly variable genes (Seurat flavour); PCA to 30 components (ARPACK); 15-neighbour graph in expression space; diffusion map to 15 components; diffusion pseudotime rooted at the nerve-associated spot with the highest composite nerve score, degenerating to the spot nearest a nerve source when a sample has no called nerve spot. **The physical distance field is not used at any step.** All computation is sparse end to end; the neighbour graph uses the `gauss` backend, because the default `umap`/pynndescent backend fails on this platform.

**Results.** The root spot fell inside the 0–50 µm ring in 43 of 43 informative samples (the one sample with no called nerve spot roots at the nearest-to-nerve spot). Spearman correlation between pseudotime and physical distance was positive in 32 of 43 samples, median ρ = +0.203; cross-sample inference by Fisher-*z* and one-sample *t* test gives *t* = 4.23, *P* = 1.2 × 10⁻⁴, and after partialling out log-UMI and mitochondrial fraction mean ρ = +0.188, *t* = 5.61, *P* = 1.4 × 10⁻⁶ — that is, the agreement strengthens when cellularity is removed, as expected if the axis is biological rather than a technical-density gradient.

**Readout ordering.** Along pseudotime deciles (0 = nerve-proximal root, 9 = distal), neuronal/Schwann, pericyte, myofibroblastic CAF and inflammatory/developmental CAF scores, together with EMT, TGF-β, collagen and ECM-organisation scores, peak in the nerve-proximal deciles; B-cell, plasma-cell, T-cell, NK, dendritic-cell and hypoxia scores peak in the distal deciles.

Source table: `TableS11_pseudotime_per_sample.csv`. Figure: Figure 4A–4B.

**Interpretation and limits.** Because the trajectory is built without spatial information, its agreement with physical distance is evidence that the perineural niche has a *direction*, not merely a gradient. Two caveats. First, diffusion pseudotime is a descriptive ordering, not a dynamical model; it does not establish that spots "progress" along the axis. Second, the root is chosen using the nerve score, which is derived from the calling markers — the root is therefore nerve-anchored by construction, while everything downstream of it is not. The informative quantity is the *correlation of the resulting ordering with distance*, which never used distance.
